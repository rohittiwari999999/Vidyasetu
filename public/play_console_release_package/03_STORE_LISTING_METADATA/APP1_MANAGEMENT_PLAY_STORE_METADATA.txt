App Name (30 characters max):
VidyaSetu School Management

Short Description (80 characters max):
School ERP & Attendance app for Indian Principals, Managers & Teachers.

Full Description (4000 characters max):
VidyaSetu Staff is a comprehensive, cloud-native School Management Application built specifically for Indian Schools (Playgroup, Nursery, Kindergarten, Primary, Secondary, and Senior Secondary Classes 1 to 12). 

Fully aligned with CBSE, ICSE, and State Board administrative norms.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS FOR PRINCIPALS, MANAGERS & TEACHERS:

1. PENDING ADMISSION APPROVAL WORKFLOW
When students or parents register online via the mobile app, their records enter a secure "Pending Approvals" verification queue. Principals and Class Teachers can review details, verify Transfer Certificates (TC), and assign official Roll Numbers and SRN (Student Registration Number) with a single tap.

2. DIGITAL ATTENDANCE REGISTER
Take daily roll call in under 30 seconds for your assigned class. The app automatically dispatches instant SMS alerts and FCM notifications to parents of absent or late students. Real-time class percentages are synchronized to help monitor CBSE's mandatory 75% attendance rule.

3. HOMEWORK & WORKSHEET ASSIGNMENT MODULE
Create and publish homework with attachments, deadlines, and maximum marks. Subject teachers can inspect student PDF submissions, grade marks, and deliver personalized feedback directly to students.

4. URGENT BROADCAST NOTICES & CIRCULARS
Broadcast urgent notices for rain emergencies, winter vacations, examination schedules, or Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM) straight to parent lockscreens using Firebase Cloud Messaging.

5. INTEGRATED VIRTUAL CLASSROOM (JITSI / WEBRTC)
Host encrypted live video classes with screen sharing, participant list, and hand-raise features. Zero external downloads needed for students.

6. FEE COLLECTION MONITORING
Track real-time fee dues, fee heads (tuition, transport, laboratory, exam fees), and collection velocity in Indian Rupees (₹).
