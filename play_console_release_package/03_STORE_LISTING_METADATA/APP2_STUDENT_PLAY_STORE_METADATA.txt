App Name (30 characters max):
VidyaSetu Student & Parent

Short Description (80 characters max):
Homework, live classes, attendance, fee receipts & report card for students.

Full Description (4000 characters max):
VidyaSetu Student & Parent app connects Indian school students and parents directly to their school (from Playgroup, Nursery, KG to 10th Board and 12th Senior Secondary).

FEATURES FOR STUDENTS & PARENTS:

1. HOMEWORK & ASSIGNMENTS
View daily homework assignments assigned by your class teacher. Download question worksheets and upload handwritten solutions in high-resolution PDF format. Receive grades and personalized teacher remarks directly on your phone.

2. LIVE ONLINE CLASSES
Join live lectures directly with a single tap. Crystal-clear video and audio powered by integrated WebRTC/Jitsi conferencing with raising hand and teacher whiteboard capabilities.

3. MONTHLY ATTENDANCE CALENDAR
Monitor your attendance daily with an interactive monthly calendar. Color-coded green (Present), red (Absent), yellow (Late), and blue (Holidays) indicators help you maintain the CBSE mandatory 75% attendance threshold for board examinations.

4. REAL-TIME NOTICE BOARD & EMERGENCY CIRCULARS
Get instant notifications for weather-related holidays, Deepawali breaks, exam routines, and PTM schedules.

5. INSTANT FEE PAYMENTS & OFFICIAL CBSE RECEIPTS
Pay school term fees securely via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or NetBanking. Instantly download official CBSE-affiliated fee acknowledgement receipts with transaction IDs.

6. DIGITAL REPORT CARDS & MARKSHEETS
View Term 1, Mid-Term, and Final report cards with CBSE grading (A1, A2, B1, B2...) and subject breakdown.

7. CLASS TIMETABLE
Check your daily 8-period routine with period timings, subject teachers, and classroom allocations.
