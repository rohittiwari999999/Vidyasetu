VidyaSetu Privacy Policy

DPDP ACT (INDIA) & GOOGLE PLAY FAMILIES POLICY COMPLIANT

Effective Date: October 2026

Applicable to: VidyaSetu School Management App (in.vidyasetu.management) & VidyaSetu Student App (in.vidyasetu.student)

1. Overview

VidyaSetu provides enterprise school management and parent communication software for Indian schools (Playgroup to 12th Senior Secondary). We are committed to safeguarding the personal and academic data of minors, parents, and educators in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India) and Google Play Store Developer Policies.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Student Profile: Name, class, section, date of birth, blood group, roll number, admission number (SRN).
  • Academic Records: Homework submissions, marks cards, daily attendance registers, and timetable.
  • Parent/Guardian Information: Parent name, registered mobile number (+91), email address, and residential address.
  • Device & Media Permissions: Camera & Microphone (strictly during active live video classes or scanning homework documents), and Device Push Tokens for emergency weather alerts.

3. Purpose of Processing

All data collected is used solely for educational purposes: conducting digital roll call, compiling CBSE marks cards, facilitating online homework, and sending automated SMS alerts to parents.

4. Data Protection & Security

All data is transmitted via secure TLS 1.3 encryption and stored in Google Cloud / Firebase data centers with AES-256 encryption at rest. We NEVER sell or monetize student data, and our apps contain zero third-party advertisements.

5. Parental Consent & Rights

Parents may inspect their child's records, request corrections, or request account deactivation through the school administration or by contacting our Data Protection Officer.
